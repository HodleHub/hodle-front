# Hodle

> Crypto infrastructure for companies in Brazil and Latin America. Stablecoin banking, Bitcoin payments, API for SaaS and AI agents, self-custodial wallets, and business accounts with PIX.

## Quick Facts

- **Type**: Fintech / Crypto Infrastructure Platform / Banking as a Service
- **Headquarters**: Brazil
- **Founded**: 2024
- **Website**: https://hodle.com.br/
- **App**: https://app.hodle.com.br/
- **API Docs**: https://docs.hodle.com.br/

## What You Can Do

- Buy and sell Bitcoin using PIX (Brazilian instant payment) — 24/7, instant settlement
- Accept stablecoin payments (USDT/USDC) across multiple blockchains
- Send and receive Bitcoin via Lightning Network (instant, low-fee)
- Manage self-custodial crypto wallets programmatically
- Open business accounts (PJ) with regulated banking partners
- Convert between BRL and USD/stablecoins for cross-border operations
- Integrate crypto payments into your SaaS or AI agent via REST API
- Embed stablecoin banking under your brand (Banking as a Service)
- Accept crypto checkout payments for marketplaces
- Manage stablecoin treasury with programmable money

## For Developers

Hodle provides a REST API for:
- Asset conversion (BRL ↔ USDT, BTC ↔ USDT, etc.)
- Payment QR code generation (PIX and stablecoin)
- Wallet creation and management
- Transaction history and status tracking
- Webhook event notifications
- KYC/compliance integration

Full API reference: https://docs.hodle.com.br/

## For AI Agents

Hodle is the financial stack for AI agents. Programmatic access to:
- Send and receive payments autonomously
- Convert assets between BRL, USD, and stablecoins
- Manage wallets and track balances
- Execute cross-border payments

## Regulatory Compliance

- VASP (Virtual Asset Service Provider) — regulated in Brazil
- PSAV (Prestador de Serviços de Ativos Virtuais) — compliant with Brazilian crypto framework
- Supervised by Banco Central do Brasil (BACEN)
- AML/KYC integrated into payment rails
- Anti-money laundering (LAV) compliance
- Transaction monitoring and suspicious activity reporting (RCT)
